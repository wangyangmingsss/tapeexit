# Protocol Pilot Plan

Status: prepared offer and research protocol. No partner has been contacted, enrolled or charged. No customer, price acceptance or revenue is claimed.

## Target And Offer

The first target is a team operating the configured X Layer V3 deployment, with direct NFT holders and reviewed standard assets. A candidate must confirm deployment identity and ownership model; vaults, staking wrappers and other forks require separate work.

The proposed service is a protocol-funded adapter review, release publication, two gateway setup, recovery package and scheduled fault rehearsal. The base tool remains open source. There is no user emergency exit fee, custody or guarantee of uninterrupted availability. This offer is a hypothesis until a team accepts a scoped pilot.

## First Conversation

Record consent and a pseudonymous organization ID locally; do not commit names, contact details or recordings to this public repository. Ask for a specific prior frontend incident, its duration, affected tasks, workaround and the team's cost of maintaining that workaround. Ask which assets and user ownership patterns are needed, who approves an adapter, who owns a publication wallet and who can fund maintenance.

Do not begin with a product pitch and then count enthusiasm as demand. A concrete willingness to allocate engineering time, an approved budget or a signed scoped pilot should be recorded separately from a general favorable comment.

## Bounded Pilot

1. Agree the manager/factory, two candidate asset contracts, task and environment in writing.
2. Review exact source, dependency findings, asset governance, release digest and ownership constraints.
3. Set publication, activation, hosting and reviewer budgets. Separate observations from quotations and authorized limits.
4. Complete actual origin and target extension wallet acceptance; a CLI wallet or synthetic provider cannot substitute.
5. Run the same task with the normal entry, blocked original frontend, blocked primary gateway and unavailable hosting using the recovery package. Record effective blocks, RPC allowances, wallet requests, receipt and remaining position.
6. Compare the same task and outage against the team's existing alternative. Randomize or counterbalance order and record assistance rather than teaching a successful task first.
7. Produce a version-bound report, open issues, maintenance owner and a go/no-go decision. Retain the protocol's permission before attributing or quoting it.

## Success And Stop Conditions

Before recruiting, agree the required completion rate, assistance threshold, target task time and acceptable maintenance cost; leave them unset until agreed. Every participant must identify the environment correctly. No wrong recipient, new approval, unreviewed asset or automatic resubmission is acceptable. Any transaction identity conflict, unsupported behavior or unknown submission stops the funds task.

Separate completed tasks, failed tasks, dropouts, repeat sessions and unique participants. Report the comparison scope and shared dependencies. A local fork, controlled fixture or simulated completion is labeled accordingly. A failed pilot remains useful evidence and must not be rewritten as success.

## Cost And Pricing

Run `npm run model:business -- <private-assumptions.json>`. The example leaves every numeric assumption null; unknown costs are not zero. Monetary inputs are USD cents and time inputs are integer minutes. The report calculates fixed monthly costs, recurring contribution, onboarding contribution and operating break-even from explicit assumptions only. It never supplies a recommended price or claims demand.

Obtain actual reviewer and hosting quotations; measure maintenance and support minutes; observe publication and activation fees. Convert chain fees using a separately recorded date and FX observation. Keep setup revenue, recurring revenue and costs distinct. Exclude unsponsored or unknown costs from any assertion of profitability.
